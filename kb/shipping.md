# Shipping and Delivery
## Delivery options
Standard shipping (3-5 business days) is free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99. Express shipping (1-2 business days) costs $14.99. Next-day delivery is available in major US cities for $24.99 when ordered before 1pm local time.
## International delivery
We ship to Canada, the UK and the EU. International delivery takes 7-14 business days. Import duties are paid by the customer on delivery.
## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships and is also shown on the Orders page. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after shipping.
## Late or lost packages
If tracking has not updated for 5 business days or the package is 5 days past the estimated date, we open a carrier investigation and ship a replacement or refund once it is confirmed lost.
## Changing the delivery address
The shipping address can be changed while the order is Processing. After it ships, we can request a carrier redirect but it is not guaranteed.
