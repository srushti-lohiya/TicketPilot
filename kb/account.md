# Account
## Creating an account
Select Sign up, enter your email and a password of at least 10 characters, then confirm the email we send. Accounts are free.
## Registration problems
If sign-up fails, check that the email is not already registered, disable ad-blockers for the checkout page, and try another browser. Disposable email addresses are not accepted.
## Editing account details
Name, email, phone number and password can be changed in Account > Profile. Changing the email requires confirming the new address.
## Password reset
Select Forgot password on the login page. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check spam or ask support to resend it.
## Switching accounts
You can link up to 3 accounts under Account > Linked accounts and switch between them from the profile menu without logging out.
## Deleting an account
Account deletion is permanent and removes order history and saved addresses within 30 days, as required by privacy law. Request it in Account > Privacy > Delete account. Open orders must be completed first.
