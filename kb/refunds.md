# Refund Policy
## Eligibility
Items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Final-sale items, gift cards and personalised products cannot be refunded.
## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or stops working within 30 days, you get a full refund or free replacement, including shipping costs. Send a photo of the damage with your order number.
## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once we receive the return, refunds are processed within 3 business days. Banks then take 5-10 business days to show the money on your statement.
## Tracking a refund
You receive an email when the refund is issued. If it has been more than 10 business days since that email, contact support with your order number and we will send the bank reference number.
