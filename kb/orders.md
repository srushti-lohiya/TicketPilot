# Orders
## Placing an order
Add items to your cart, open the cart and select Checkout. Guests can check out without an account. Orders are confirmed by email within a few minutes.
## Changing an order
Orders can be changed (items, sizes, quantity) until they are packed, usually within 2 hours of purchase. After packing, the order cannot be edited; you can return unwanted items after delivery.
## Cancelling an order
Orders with status Processing can be cancelled free of charge from the Orders page or by support. Orders that have shipped cannot be cancelled; refuse the delivery or return them for a refund.
## Order status meanings
Processing: payment received, not packed yet. Packed: being prepared, can no longer be edited. Shipped: on the way with a tracking number. Delivered: carrier confirmed delivery.
