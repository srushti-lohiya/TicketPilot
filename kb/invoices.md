# Invoices and Receipts
## Getting an invoice
Every order has a PDF invoice under Orders > Order details > Download invoice. Business customers can add a company name and VAT number in Account settings before ordering; invoices cannot be re-issued with a different company name afterwards.
## Invoice discrepancies
The invoice shows item prices, discounts, shipping and tax. If the amount charged differs from the invoice, it is usually tax or currency conversion by your bank. Support can verify the charge with the order number.
