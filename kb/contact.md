# Contacting Support
## Support channels and hours
Chat and email support are available 24/7. Phone support is available Monday to Friday, 8am-8pm Eastern Time, at 1-800-555-0199.
## Speaking to a human
Customers can ask to be transferred to a human agent at any time. Human agents reply to escalated tickets within 4 business hours, and within 1 hour for urgent tickets.
## Complaints and feedback
We take complaints seriously. Every complaint is reviewed by a team lead who replies within 1 business day. Customers can also leave product reviews on the product page after delivery.
