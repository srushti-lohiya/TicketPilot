# Payments
## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and store gift cards. Buy-now-pay-later through Klarna is available on orders between $50 and $1,000 in the US.
## Declined payments
Declines usually come from the bank's fraud check, a billing address that does not match the card, or an expired card. Check the billing address, try another method, or contact your bank. We never see or store full card numbers.
## Double charges
A duplicate charge is often a temporary authorisation that disappears within 3-5 business days. If both charges are still shown as completed after 5 days, support will refund the duplicate.
