# Newsletter and Subscriptions
## Newsletter
Sign up at the bottom of any page to receive a 10% welcome code. Unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of any email or in Account > Notifications; it takes effect within 48 hours.
## Subscription box cancellation
The monthly subscription box can be cancelled any time. There is no cancellation fee if you cancel at least 3 days before the next billing date; boxes already billed are shipped as normal. Annual plans cancelled in the first 14 days get a full refund; after that, unused months are refunded minus a $10 fee.
